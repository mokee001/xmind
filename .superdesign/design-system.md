Homepage ONLY; no onboarding, preference or settings redesign, no production App edits.

## Scene A — 流动天空
PRIMARY art direction: user's dynamic-sky reference; image-led atmospheric time experience. Use the supplied photoreal sky content asset edge to edge across the ENTIRE viewport, no opaque white calendar on top. Vast clouds, luminous daylight and optical depth are the main visual identity. Relevant craft reference: Superdesign cinematic-style for full-bleed media/quiet hierarchy only; do not borrow its marketing sections. Refero motion guidance for continuity, reduced motion and fast action feedback.
Visual lock: sky blue #4799DA, air #DCECF7, cloud #F6FBFF, deep ink #153750, sunset #E99B7D, night #101E37. Interface white/ice text on clear blue, dark-blue text over light cloud zones; carefully placed soft scrim for guaranteed readability. Helvetica Neue/PingFang SC system sans; oversized lightweight current day 92–120px with tiny month/year, mixed sizes intentional. No olive, coral pills, beige, giant marketing sentence or monochrome month grid.
Composition: fixed floating status/preferences/settings header. The scene has a huge open sky, a quiet small title 回忆日历, oversized today numeral 15 integrated into the scene, and date context 九月 · 2026 / 星期二. Show a slim vertical trajectory of days, not weekday columns. Past sample memory windows drift at different horizontal positions along this vertical journey; image-backed air-glass frames with a date tether, not a list of regular rounded cards. Today is an open moment, not a thumbnail. Lower edge invites 向上滑，回到那些日子. Past memories should start below large calm date moment, make one glimpse visible in first viewport. Today/future remain blank.
Motion lock: real cloud drift/parallax, visible smoothly changing day→sunset→night light within an optional demo clock cycle. Provide a small 明 / 暮 / 夜 or 体验一天 scene control so the user can directly judge temporal atmosphere without waiting hours; explicitly simulation, not weather history. Scroll drives date progression and past-day focus. Reading text must not drift. Pause ambient motion button. With reduced-motion, freeze drifting/parallax and change light only by direct selection. No animation blocking navigation.

## Scene B — 漂浮泳池
PRIMARY art direction: user's pool reference; tactile top-down water and architecture. Use supplied photoreal pool-water texture edge to edge with real caustics, small underwater tiles, pale stone coping at one edge. Relevant Superdesign mosaic-grid-architecture-style reference contributes physical modularity/material framing only, not its marketing grid. This must not reuse sky's timeline/hero layout or previous 7-column month grid.
Visual lock: saturated pool cyan #39C2D3, deep teal #0B6575, water dark #064B61, porcelain #F8F6EF, tiny vermilion date marker #ED674B. Rounded or condensed system numeric type, PingFang SC; bold poolside stencilled month 09 with 2026 and 回忆日历 in small architectural labels. NOT black Swiss print over gray, not blurred wallpaper behind cards.
Composition: thin translucent/porcelain utility header atop the pool scene. Along LEFT pool coping a vertical date measuring strip with 15 / 14 / 13 / 12 / 11, and small intervening ticks (no seven-column matrix). The water is a spacious scene. Two or three already-displayed memory prints (source sample illustrations acceptable, clearly example; no personal-photo access) float on water at different tilts/positions with convincing thin paper edges and water shadows. Their dates/captions are anchored to the prints. Current day is an empty sunlit circle/ripple marker with small 今天; future dates never get images. A compact pool-edge month label and discreet round 回到今天 action. Different spatial composition from sky.
Motion lock: actual moving water caustics/refraction layer, restrained floating buoyancy of memory prints, contact ripples on pointer/touch. Scroll along pool changes selected date and brings different existing sample prints through view. Crucially ripples should be visible, not merely promise text. UI labels/nav stay still. Pause animation affordance. Reduced motion disables continuous drift/refraction, click feedback becomes a brief static ring or opacity fade. No loops on nav.

## Media, typography and interaction contract
Final imagery must use supplied public uploaded asset URLs. Preserve content role: real sky/water assets dominate, no CSS blob substitutes, no neutral shell consuming the viewport. Use sample illustration thumbnails from prior draft only for historical prints; label examples, not real photo history. No logo needed because home has no logo slot. Header line icons can reuse source or equivalent accurate Lucide shapes. Touch hit areas >=44px. Foreground contrast must be checked in every phase. CSS keyframes for environment and real JS for phase/date controls are welcome; keep self-contained HTML. Proper scroll host, no horizontal overflow, persistent peer entries. This round requires a visible dynamic experience, not just a static mockup. No unimplemented UI that looks functional.

User explicitly allows changing fundamental structure; old structural locks are withdrawn. Refero research remains bundled craft and available Superdesign references; do not claim live Refero search.
